CORE ATTRACTION 01

Automatic Bluetooth Detection

You never have to connect through our app or pick your model from a list. Simply put your earphones in pairing mode, connect them in your regular Bluetooth menu, and open OmniBuds. The app automatically recognizes your exact earbuds and displays their tailored control surface upfront.

CORE ATTRACTION 02

Persistent On-Chip Hardware Memory

Any setting you adjust—touch button actions, Active Noise Cancellation, or sound profiles—is written directly into your earbuds' onboard flash memory (EEPROM). If you disable a button or customize a double-tap, it stays saved in the earbuds forever. Even if you connect them to your laptop, iPad, or gym TV, your custom configuration remains active on the hardware!
