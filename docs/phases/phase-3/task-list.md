## The thing that changed during execution: Phase 2's receiver flag

### TASK-P3-009 — Find the Phase 2 receiver-flag defect, and fix it from documentation rather than waiting for a handset
**Objective.** Settle ADR-P3-011's parked question against the platform's own receiver-export guidance, and correct shipped code that the answer condemns.
**Dependencies.** TASK-P3-006. **Owner.** D, I.

**Implementation notes.** What went wrong, in the phase's own words: `ACTION_STATE_CHANGED` and its Bluetooth cousins are *protected* broadcasts — verified against AOSP `core/res/AndroidManifest.xml` at tag `android-15.0.0_r11` — and protection governs who may **send**; the export flag governs who may **reach** a receiver; the two facts had been read as one, and lint's message was quoted back as permission. **Status.** `done — corrected, unverified` — evidence: the two register functions read as described; `docs/phases/phase-2/validation.md` carries the correction paragraph beginning "Corrected during Phase 3, and the correction is the more important half of this note"; `lintDebug` → `No issues found.` (measured). **This is the first of the two execution-time findings this phase records rather than hides: a defect in committed, pushed Phase 2 behaviour, found by reading a documentation page, fixed before any device was consulted.**
