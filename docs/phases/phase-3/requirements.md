### REQ-P3-002 — The platform link, the bond and the observability of a device are three independent axes, none defaulting to another's value
**Description.** `DeviceConnectionState` SHALL carry only the link {`UNKNOWN, CONNECTING, CONNECTED, DISCONNECTING, DISCONNECTED`}, `DeviceBondState` only the pairing {`UNKNOWN, NONE, BONDING, BONDED`}, `DeviceAvailability` only whether the platform would report the device at all {`UNKNOWN, AVAILABLE, UNAVAILABLE`}; none of the three SHALL have a default, no member SHALL be coerced into `state.ConnectionState`, and `UNKNOWN` SHALL never be rendered as `DISCONNECTED`, `NONE` or `UNAVAILABLE`. A bonded-and-disconnected device SHALL be expressible without a compound member.
**Rationale.** Prompt §6 makes the paired-versus-connected split mandatory and lists the seven conceptual states; ADR-P3-001 explains why one flattened enum cannot say which of its halves moved, why the session type is not the link type, and why `UNAVAILABLE` describes our view rather than the link; master §53.
**Dependencies.** REQ-P3-001; ADR-P3-001, ADR-P0-016.
**Acceptance criteria.** AC-1 the three enums hold exactly those members. AC-2 `isConnected()` is true only for `CONNECTED`, `isProvablyDisconnected()` only for `DISCONNECTED`, `isUnsettled()` for the three unsettled values, `isKnown()` for everything but `UNKNOWN`. AC-3 `isBonded()` is true only on a positive `BONDED` and `UNKNOWN` is never read as unpaired. AC-4 `isObservable()` is true only for `AVAILABLE` and `UNAVAILABLE` is not `isUnread()`. AC-5 the three are distinct types and neither is `ConnectionState`. AC-6 one device record can hold `BONDED` + `DISCONNECTED` + `AVAILABLE` at once, and a bond announcement moves the bond axis and nothing else. AC-7 the paired record type carries the bond axis and *nothing* else — no link, no profile set, no availability — so the distinction is structural rather than a predicate a consumer must remember to call.
**Verification.** AC-1/AC-2 `platform/DeviceObservationVocabularyTest.unknownConnectionStateIsNeverReadAsDisconnected` (`DeviceObservationVocabularyTest.kt:71`), `.onlyConnectedCountsAsConnectedAndOnlyDisconnectedCountsAsProvenDown` (`:81`) against `DeviceConnectionState.kt:22-49`; AC-3 `.bondStateUnknownIsNeverReadAsUnpaired` (`:102`) against `DeviceBondState.kt:16-34`; AC-4 `.availabilityRefusalIsDistinctFromAnUnreadField` (`:110`) against `DeviceAvailability.kt:16-41`; AC-5 `.theThreeAxesAreIndependentTypesWithNoCoercion` (`:118`), which asserts the three `Class` objects are distinct and that `ConnectionState` is not among them; AC-6 by `platform/ConnectedDeviceObserverTest.theThreeAxesOfOneDeviceMoveIndependently` (`ConnectedDeviceObserverTest.kt:44`), `.aBondAnnouncementMovesTheBondAxisAndSaysNothingAboutTheLink` at platform level (`AndroidConnectedDeviceSourceTest.kt:527`) and `BondedDeviceObservationTest.aBondRoundCannotMakeADeviceTheUnionNeverListedLookDisconnected` (`BondedDeviceObservationTest.kt:242`); AC-7 by `BondedDeviceObservationTest.thePairedRecordHasNoFieldThatCouldClaimALink` (`:365`), which reflects over `BondedDeviceObservation`'s declared properties and fails if a link-, profile- or availability-bearing field is ever added, and `.theBondListCreditsTheBondAxisAndLeavesEveryOtherFieldAlone` (`:215`), which asserts the same through the one fold that may touch a projection record from this side (`DeviceObservation.creditingBond`, `DeviceObservation.kt:159-160`). **Inspection residue:** the upward direction — that no later phase maps these into `ConnectionState` — is forbidden for *imports* by the layer rule and is not otherwise checked; ADR-P3-001 assigns that translation to Phase 4.

### REQ-P3-005 — A profile that could not answer contributes no device, withdraws no link, and never turns the union into an empty room
**Description.** Before enumerating, the source SHALL ask which profiles are answerable and SHALL enumerate only those; `ProfileObservationSupport` {`UNKNOWN, ANSWERABLE, NOT_ANSWERABLE`} SHALL carry the answer, with a refused or absent binding mapped to `NOT_ANSWERABLE` and an in-flight or unrequested binding to `UNKNOWN`; `ProfileSupportReport` SHALL default an unasked profile to `UNKNOWN`; a profile that declined SHALL contribute no record and withdraw none; and a `Success` carrying no device from a union in which nothing answered SHALL be refused by the engine as `RESOURCE_UNAVAILABLE`, never published as an empty list.
**Rationale.** Prompt §8 ("Do not assume Android provides a universal broadcast … Do not assume `BluetoothProfile` APIs expose every connected device") and §14; ADR-P3-008's per-profile rule; ADR-P3-015 rules 4 and 7 — rule 7 being the case the orchestrator re-classified from `UNSUPPORTED_OPERATION` to `RESOURCE_UNAVAILABLE` on review, because the operation is supported and `NEVER_RETRY` would instruct a caller to abandon a stack that was merely not ready.
**Dependencies.** REQ-P3-004, REQ-P3-013; ADR-P3-008, ADR-P3-015.
**Acceptance criteria.** AC-1 a declined profile is never enumerated and contributes nothing. AC-2 a profile still awaiting its callback reports `UNKNOWN`, not refusal. AC-3 a profile the running OS does not expose reports `NOT_ANSWERABLE` without asking the handset. AC-4 an unasked profile reads `UNKNOWN` through `supportFor`. AC-5 a silence from a non-answerable profile cannot disconnect a device it had claimed. **Verification.** AC-1/AC-3 `AndroidConnectedDeviceSourceTest.aProfileThatDeclinedContributesNothingAndIsNeverEnumerated` (`:185`), `.aProfileTheRunningOsDoesNotExposeIsDeclinedWithoutAskingTheHandset` (`:226`); AC-2 `.aProfileStillWaitingForItsCallbackIsUnknownRatherThanDeclined` (`:209`), and the ladder that decides which of the two silences a multi-channel answer reports is now itself a test file — `ProfileAnswerabilityTest.aBindStillInFlightOutvotesAChannelThatWasRefused` (`ProfileAnswerabilityTest.kt:20`), `.theOrderingHoldsWhicheverOrderTheChannelsReportedIn` (`:29`), `.aRefusalStillOutvotesAChannelThatNeverAsked` (`:44`), `.theLadderIsNotTheDeclarationOrderThatWouldRankARefusalAboveAPendingBind` (`:69`); AC-6 `.aUnionWhereNothingAnswersReachesTheEngineAsNoAnswersAndNotAsNoDevices` (`:167`) and `ConnectedDeviceObserverTest.aUnionWhereNothingAnsweredCannotReportAnEmptyRoom` (`:380`), which asserts the category, the retained `unansweredProfiles` and `isUnionComplete == false`; AC-5 `ConnectedDeviceObserverTest.aSilentProfileCannotWithdrawALinkItNeverRestated` (`:404`), which runs the same second round against a complete union in the same method and gets the opposite answer. **AC-4 is read, not tested:** the three report helpers in `FakeConnectedDeviceSource.kt:150-169` always build a full map over `enumerationUnion`, so `ProfileSupportReport.supportFor`'s absent-profile fallback is exercised by no test — `architecture-audit.md` §6's "an unprobed entry reads as unknown" holds for this type by construction only.
