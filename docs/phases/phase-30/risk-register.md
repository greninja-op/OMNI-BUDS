# Phase 30 — Risk Register

## R-30-01: Tests prove too much
**Likelihood:** Medium | **Impact:** High
**Mitigation:** Evidence levels; simulated passes never upgrade
capability verification.

## R-30-02: Fixtures mistaken for captures
**Likelihood:** Medium | **Impact:** High
**Mitigation:** Provenance explicit; synthetic default; consent rule.

## R-30-03: Flaky tests
**Likelihood:** Medium | **Impact:** Medium
**Mitigation:** Determinism classification; seeded injection; isolation.

## R-30-04: Production backdoors
**Likelihood:** Low | **Impact:** High
**Mitigation:** Test-only interfaces; injector never in production.

## R-30-05: Untested critical paths
**Likelihood:** Medium | **Impact:** Medium
**Mitigation:** Gaps documented; hardware campaigns in Phases 31–33.
