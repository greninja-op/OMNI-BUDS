## 2. Enums, every member

### 2.1 `SessionEngineStage` — where the *engine* is, three members
`session/SessionVocabulary.kt:13-22`, declaration order `NOT_STARTED, RUNNING, STOPPED`. `NOT_STARTED` "created, nothing applied" (`:15`), `RUNNING` "applying published projections" (`:18`), `STOPPED` "ended by request. Sessions were cleared with a reason, and a restart is legal" (`:21`). What each is forbidden to mean, from the type's own KDoc (`:6-11`): it is **not** a second lifecycle axis and **not** a device condition — whether the projections said anything about anybody belongs to `SessionObservationStatus`, which is why "stopped" and "unconfirmed" are two values rather than one boolean. `RUNNING` is set from one rule and one only: a projection whose `stage` is `ObservationStage.STOPPED` makes the engine `STOPPED`, anything else makes it `RUNNING` (`DeviceSessionEngine.kt:186-190`), and `terminateAll` publishes `STOPPED` unconditionally (`:214`). Pinned by `SessionStatePurityTest.kt:234-244` (size 3, exact name set, and no `ACTIVE`/`ENDED` anywhere in `ConnectionState`); observed in tests at `DeviceSessionEngineTest.kt:57` (`RUNNING` after one apply), `:268` and `:445` (`STOPPED`), `:471` (`RUNNING` again after a restart, which is legal).

### 2.2 `SessionIdentityBasis` — how confidently a session is attributed, two members
`SessionVocabulary.kt:38-44`: `KEYED` ("attributable to one device across rounds, because the platform gave a key", `:40`) and `AMBIGUOUS` ("reported by the platform with nothing to attribute it by. Never matched, never merged", `:43`). Forbidden meanings, from `:35-36`: the absence of a key is **not a ranking** — an ambiguous session is as real as a keyed one while it lasts; what it cannot do is last. Exactly two producers exist: `DeviceSessionEngine.kt:167` passes `KEYED` for a report whose `isAttributable` held (`:147`), and `:172-179` passes `AMBIGUOUS` with `DeviceObservationKey.NotReported` as the key, never a real one. Nothing in this area can change a basis after creation — `applyReport`'s KDoc says so at `:246-251` and no call site writes the field (`withState` and `withMergedIdentity` are the only transforms that touch a `TrackedDeviceSession` mid-life, `TrackedDeviceSession.kt:78-92`, and neither passes `basis`).

### 2.3 `SessionTermination` — why the engine stopped tracking, four members in order
`SessionVocabulary.kt:61-80`, declaration order and line numbers: `PROVEN_DISCONNECT_PAST_GRACE` (`:63`), `AMBIGUOUS_SESSION_EXPIRED` (`:73`), `OBSERVATION_STOPPED` (`:76`), `ENGINE_CANCELLED` (`:79`). Meanings as declared: already disconnected by a completed union and absent from the one after it (`:62`); an unattributed session reaching the end of the one projection it could exist in (`:65-72` — explicitly "**not** evidence that the device went away"); the projection stopped observing (`:75`); the engine's own caller went away (`:78`). What none of them may mean, from `:47-54`: nothing here claims to know why a *device* left — no `POWERED_OFF`, no `UNPAIRED`, asserted at `SessionStatePurityTest.kt:215-231`, which pins the four-member ordered list by name (`:218-226`), refuses `POWERED_OFF`/`UNPAIRED` (`:227`), and asserts `ABSENT_FROM_COMPLETE_UNION` is **not** among it (`:230`). The KDoc at `:49` reads "Four reasons", which now matches the four members — an earlier draft of this file flagged that sentence as stale while the enum still carried a fifth (`ABSENT_FROM_COMPLETE_UNION`), but that member has been deleted (ADR-P4-002's close-out correction), so the count is accurate and the flag is withdrawn. ⚠ Reading-only: the type KDoc at `:56` still names `[ABSENT_FROM_COMPLETE_UNION]` and explains it as though it were a member ("says the phone enumerated every profile it could"), which is now a dead reference (§18.5, §17.3.6). Absence is a *disconnect*, carried as `DisconnectEvidence.ABSENT_FROM_COMPLETE_UNION` (§2.4), never a termination — the reason the enum member was removed and why ADR-P4-006 lists four reasons.

### 2.4 `DisconnectEvidence` — how a disconnect was established, two members
`DeviceSessionEvent.kt:107-113`: `REPORTED` ("the platform positively reported no link", `:109`) and `ABSENT_FROM_COMPLETE_UNION` ("absent from a union in which every profile answered", `:112`). One producer each: `DeviceSessionEngine.kt:288` for a `DeviceConnectionState.DISCONNECTED` report, `:360` for the absence path. There is deliberately no third member for "absent from a round that did not answer", because that case produces no disconnect at all (`applyAbsence`'s `else -> session`, `:377`), and no member for "inferred from silence past a timer", because there is no timer (§9).

### 2.5 `SessionObservationStatus` — what the engine is entitled to believe, five cases
`SessionObservationStatus.kt:36-91`, a sealed interface: `data object Unread` (`:38`, "nothing has been applied yet"), `data object Unconfirmed` (`:44`, "the projection carries devices, but no round has completed"), `data class Confirmed(val unionComplete: Boolean)` (`:54`), `data class Refused(val error: OmniBudsError)` (`:63`), `data object Stopped` (`:66`). Forbidden meanings, each stated in the file: `Unread` belongs to the engine before it has seen anything and is **never** derived from a projection (`of` cannot return it, `:82-90`, asserted at `SessionStatePurityTest.kt:129-130`); `Refused` is not a cleared list (ADR-P4-007); `Stopped` is neither a failure nor an empty room; `Confirmed(unionComplete = false)` is not a census of anybody. A cancellation has **no case here**, by the rule at `:31-34` — a called-off read is not a finding, and deriving a status from a projection cannot invent one.

`isEmptyMeaningful` (`:72-73`) is `this is Confirmed && unionComplete`, and it is the only member of the type with a rule rather than a value. `of(snapshot)` (`:82-90`) reads `snapshot.deviceRoundRefusal` once (`:83`) and then, in the precedence order the KDoc at `:77-81` states — an ended observation outranks a refusal, a refusal outranks a completed round, a completed round outranks unsettled announcements — returns in this order:

```kotlin
snapshot.stage == ObservationStage.STOPPED -> Stopped                     // :85
refusal != null                            -> Refused(refusal)            // :86
snapshot.restsOnCompletedRound             -> Confirmed(snapshot.isUnionComplete)  // :87
else                                       -> Unconfirmed                 // :88
```

The order is load-bearing, and one test walks it: `observationStatusPrecedenceKeepsTheFourCasesExclusive` (`SessionStatePurityTest.kt:120-143`) asserts that a **stopped** projection carrying a refusal derives `Stopped`, not `Refused` (`:131-133`) — so a grant revoked while the observer shut down reports as stopped, not as permission-denied — and that `Confirmed(unionComplete = false)` is not `isEmptyMeaningful` (`:140-142`).

### 2.6 `reachableStates` — the states this engine will accept as a target, six members
`TrackedDeviceSession.kt:125-128`, derived rather than listed:

```kotlin
val reachableStates: Set<ConnectionState> = ConnectionStateTransitions
    .allowedNext(ConnectionState.UNKNOWN)
    .filter { state -> state != ConnectionState.ERROR }
    .toSet() + ConnectionState.UNKNOWN
```

`allowedNext(UNKNOWN)` is `state/ConnectionState.kt:100-101` — the `UNKNOWN` row of `forward` (`:38-44`: `DISCOVERED, PAIRED, CONNECTED, DISCONNECTED, TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE`) plus `ERROR` plus the state itself — so the declared set is exactly `{UNKNOWN, DISCOVERED, PAIRED, CONNECTED, TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE, DISCONNECTED}`, asserted by name and by equality at `SessionStatePurityTest.kt:44-55`, with the four excluded members named in a set (`:37-42`) and `ERROR`'s absence pinned separately (`:56-57`). Deriving the set from the table rather than writing it beside it is the reason the commit message calls this "cannot silently drift": a Phase 1 edit to the `UNKNOWN` row changes the guard's expectation in the same commit. What the set does **not** claim: that these are the only states `DeviceState` can hold — `attemptConnection` accepts any state `canTransition` allows (`DeviceState.kt:113`), which includes `ERROR` from every state (`ConnectionState.kt:32, 101`), and `IDENTIFYING → CAPABILITY_DISCOVERY → READY → CONTROL_SESSION` remain legal moves of the Phase 1 table (`:54-80`). The claim is narrower and honest: **no engine path requests one** (§4's `targetStateOf` codomain has no `ERROR`, and `createSession` starts from `UNKNOWN`, `DeviceSessionEngine.kt:407`).

## 3. State machines

### 3.1 The session's connection machine, and the one door
Every move goes through `TrackedDeviceSession.moving` (`:117-122`) into `DeviceState.attemptConnection` (`DeviceState.kt:109-129`) into `ConnectionStateTransitions.canTransition` (`state/ConnectionState.kt:103-104`). A legal move returns `OperationOutcome.Success` with a new `DeviceState` carrying `connection = next, revision = revision + 1` (`DeviceState.kt:122-128`); an illegal one returns `OperationOutcome.Failure(OmniBudsError(INVALID_STATE, "device-state.attemptConnection", "cannot move session state from $connection to $next"))` (`:114-120`) and the state object is untouched — pinned by `SessionStatePurityTest.kt:107-117`, which checks the category *and* that the record still says `CONNECTED`. Nothing in Phase 4 throws on a refusal and nothing swallows one: the three call sites are `DeviceSessionEngine.kt:274-304` (record it, keep the session, `:302`), `:352-369` (record it, keep the session, `:367`) and `:412-423` (record it and still create the session at `UNKNOWN`, `:414-420`).

Within the reachable set, the moves this table has no row for are the ones the engine discovers rather than predicts. `CONNECTED → PAIRED` is illegal (`ConnectionState.kt:54-59` has no `PAIRED`), `CONNECTED → UNKNOWN` is illegal on the same list, while `TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE → UNKNOWN` **is** legal (`:87-91`) and `DISCONNECTED → UNKNOWN` is legal (`:81-86`). That asymmetry is a consequence of Phase 1's table, not a Phase 4 choice, and §4 records which branch of `targetStateOf` it lands on.

### 3.2 The engine's own machine: `NOT_STARTED → RUNNING ⇄ STOPPED → RUNNING …`
`DeviceSessionSnapshot.notStarted()` starts at `NOT_STARTED` (`DeviceSessionSnapshot.kt:61-68`, and `DeviceSessionEngine.kt:88` seeds the flow with it); `apply` moves to `RUNNING` unless the projection it read had already stopped (`:186-190`); `terminateAll` and `stop()` move to `STOPPED` (`:214`, `:201-203`). `STOPPED` is not terminal — `anEngineReusedAfterAStopStartsFreshSessionsRatherThanResumingGhostOnes` (`DeviceSessionEngineTest.kt:462-472`) applies a projection after a completed `consume` and gets one session, `CONNECTED`, `RUNNING`, with `tracked` having been emptied at `:212`. The machine carries no device meaning, which is §2.1's point restated as behaviour.

### 3.3 The grace machine, stated as the code states it
One predicate and one field, no counter and no clock comparison: `TrackedDeviceSession.isAwaitingGraceExpiry` is exactly `connectionState == ConnectionState.DISCONNECTED` (`:70-71`) — a single condition, because a terminated session is dropped in the same round its `SessionEnded` publishes (ADR-P4-006), so there is no `termination` flag left to test (§5.1, §18.7). `timeline.disconnectedAtEpochMillis` is set by `markedDisconnected` (`:99-100` → `SessionTimeline.kt:56-57`), which keeps the **first** mark (`?:`). The full sequence, with the tests, is §9.

## 4. ADR-P4-004's mapping table, as implemented behaviour

One function owns the whole translation: `private fun targetStateOf(report: DeviceObservation, tracked: Boolean): ConnectionState?` (`DeviceSessionEngine.kt:480-501`; KDoc `:471-479`). Its shape is the ADR's shape — observability first, then the link axis, with the bond axis consulted only where the link axis is silent — and the `tracked` parameter is what makes ADR-P4-004's "for a tracked device … no session created otherwise" expressible without a second table. Codomain: `CONNECTED`, `DISCOVERED`, `TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE`, `DISCONNECTED`, `PAIRED`, `UNKNOWN`, and `null`. Not `ERROR`, not `IDENTIFYING`, not `CAPABILITY_DISCOVERY`, not `READY`, not `CONTROL_SESSION`.

**The null return, and the case it prevents.** `targetStateOf` returns `null` from three branches — `:482`, `:491-492`, `:498` — and `createSession` turns each one into "no session" before an id is minted (`:395`, `?: return null`). What that prevents is the specific overclaim the Phase 3 census made tempting: a device the phone can *see* but cannot *report a link for* — bonded, enumerated, remembered — would otherwise enter `activeSessions` history as a session whose first state is `DISCONNECTED`, and a UI would show a disconnect this app never witnessed and a pairing this phase is forbidden to read as a connection (prompt §10.B, ADR-P3-017). The test proves the census-and-negation half of it directly: after one round containing a `DISCONNECTED` record and an unobservable record, `sessions` is empty **and** `isEmptyMeaningful` is true (`DeviceSessionEngineTest.kt:352-353`) — an empty session list from an answered union is a finding, not a gap. The second consequence is bookkeeping: `revision` still advances and the round is still published (`DeviceSessionEngine.kt:181-191`), so declining to open a session is a decision recorded in the value rather than a silent drop.

**Where the table and the fold differ.** `applyReport` calls `targetStateOf(report, tracked = true)` (`:258`) and, on `null`, keeps the session exactly as it was and returns it (`?: return session`) — for a tracked device `null` is unreachable by construction (`:481-482`, `:488-490`, `:497` all branch on `tracked`), so the `return session` is the defensive half, not a policy. Creation calls `targetStateOf(report, tracked = false)` (`:395`). Absence never calls `targetStateOf` at all: it reads `SessionObservationStatus` instead, which is why rows 7-9 are phrased as round properties rather than device properties.

**Where two records share a key in one projection.** The partition is a `LinkedHashMap` assignment (`DeviceSessionEngine.kt:144, 148`), so a second record with the same key **overwrites** the first rather than merging with it. This is not a Phase 4 invention of a worse merge: Phase 3 already folds same-key reports into one `DeviceObservation` by rank before publishing (`ConnectedDeviceObserver.kt:753-759`, `DeviceObservation.mergedWith`), so in any projection the engine is meant to receive, the two are already one record. The last-writer rule only ever decides what happens against a producer that skipped its own merge, and the observable outcome — one session, one id — is what `oneDeviceReportedTwiceInOneRoundStillOpensOneSession` pins (`DeviceSessionEngineTest.kt:75-84`); ⚠ which record's *state* wins is not asserted anywhere. See §18.16.

Companion: `moving` (`:117-122`) and `reachableStates` (`:125-128`, §2.6); `mapState` (`:130-136`) is private and is the only place an `OperationOutcome<DeviceState>` becomes an `OperationOutcome<TrackedDeviceSession>`, keeping `Failure`'s error verbatim (`:134`) — the reason `recordRefusal` receives Phase 1's own sentence (`DeviceSessionEngine.kt:302`) rather than a Phase 4 paraphrase. | `snapshot: StateFlow` | `MutableStateFlow(notStarted())` + `asStateFlow()` (`:88`, `:91`) | rule 5.5's "latest value matters" half, rule 5's ban on a cold flow for device state, and rule 5.7's "others read, never write" | a slow collector always converges — this is the reconciliation source, so nothing else needs a history |

| `extraBufferCapacity` | `64` (`:521`) | a declared bound; `Channel.UNLIMITED` was rejected as "the bounded window wearing a domain name" (ADR-P4-008) | it is a window, not a queue, and nothing measures how often it is reached — 64 edges is roughly eight rounds of a two-device census at this phase's largest event rate |
| `onBufferOverflow` | `DROP_OLDEST` (`:100`) | ADR-P4-008: a lagging consumer loses an old edge instead of stalling the producer | observable and documented at `:94-97` and `:25-28`; losing an edge is never losing a fact, because `snapshot` is the truth (rule 5.6, `phase-0/specs.md:146`) |

| `REFUSAL_WINDOW` | `8` (`:518`), applied as `takeLast` at `:446` | ADR-P4-007 / ADR-P3-015 rule 8: prompt §15 forbids swallowing and §10 forbids a store, so the middle path is a bounded published window; Phase 3's identical constant (`ConnectedDeviceObserver.kt:957`, consumed `:756`) is "reused rather than re-decided" (`:434-437`) | the newest 8 survive and the rest are gone — `theRefusalWindowIsBoundedAndKeepsTheNewestMoves` drives 12 refusals and asserts the size is exactly 8 (`DeviceSessionEngineTest.kt:373-385`) |

Duplicate suppression sits at the edge, not in the buffer: `applyReport` publishes only inside `if (target != current.connectionState)` (`:272`) and identity events only inside `if (merged != current.session.identity)` (`:308`), so a flapping profile cannot produce an event storm (ADR-P4-008's consequence). An identical round still republishes the `StateFlow` — the revision moves (`:182`) while no event is emitted — and `everyRoundAdvancesTheRevisionAndAnIdenticalRoundEmitsNoEvents` (`:387-401`) pins both halves: three applies of one projection give revision `+2` past the first, exactly one `SessionCreated`, zero `SessionUpdated`. That asymmetry is deliberate and worth stating plainly: **`revision` counts published rounds, not state changes**, so a consumer cannot use it as a dirty flag (§12).

## 9. The grace window, specified round by round

ADR-P4-006's rule (2): a session that reaches `DISCONNECTED` — by positive report or by absence from a complete union — is kept for **exactly one further published round**, then ended. The window is measured in published rounds, not seconds; the only state it needs is `isAwaitingGraceExpiry` (`TrackedDeviceSession.kt:70-71`). One `apply` is one round, so the sequence below is executable as written and is exactly what `DeviceSessionEngineTest` runs.

| Round | Projection | Engine path | Resulting state | Event | Termination produced | Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | device present, `link = CONNECTED` | `createSession` `:388-428` | `CONNECTED`; `startedAt` set; `disconnectedAt = null` | `SessionCreated` (`:426`) | — | `aConnectedDeviceOpensExactlyOneSession` (`:49-60`) |
| R2 | empty records over an answered union (`Confirmed(unionComplete = true)`) | `applyAbsence` `:342-364`, grace not yet held → move | `DISCONNECTED`; `disconnectedAt` set by `markedDisconnected` (`:363`); session **kept** | `SessionDisconnected(ABSENT_FROM_COMPLETE_UNION)` (`:358-362`) | — | `aDeviceAbsentFromACompleteUnionIsReportedDisconnectedNotDeleted` (`:96-105`) — asserts the session is *still there*, `isAwaitingGraceExpiry`, and `disconnectedAt` non-null |
| R3a | device present again, `CONNECTED` | `applyReport` `:278-282`: move `DISCONNECTED → CONNECTED`, then `resumed()` | `CONNECTED`, **same** `sessionId`, `disconnectedAt` cleared (`SessionTimeline.kt:60`) | `SessionReconnected` (`:281`) | — | `aDeviceThatComesBackInsideTheGraceKeepsItsSessionId` (`:169-183`) — id equality, disconnect time gone, last event asserted as the type |

The two termination reasons this window can produce are therefore `PROVEN_DISCONNECT_PAST_GRACE` (`:265` from a report, `:346` from an absence) and — for an ambiguous session, which never enters the grace because it is expired before the fold runs — `AMBIGUOUS_SESSION_EXPIRED` (`:238`). The code turns an answered absence into a *disconnect* (`DisconnectEvidence.ABSENT_FROM_COMPLETE_UNION`, `:360`) and only the following round into an *ending*, so `SessionTermination` carries no absence member at all: ADR-P4-006 now lists four reasons and states that absence is evidence of a disconnect rather than of a termination, and the enum member that used to blur the two (`SessionTermination.ABSENT_FROM_COMPLETE_UNION`) has been deleted at close-out (§2.3, §18.7 — the `DisconnectEvidence` of that name still exists and is its correct home, §2.4). A device that only goes `TEMPORARILY_UNAVAILABLE` and returns never enters the window at all — ADR-P4-006's rule (1) — because `isAwaitingGraceExpiry` tests `DISCONNECTED` alone, and `markedDisconnected` is called from two sites only (`:285`, `:363`), both reached solely with `target == DISCONNECTED`.

### 10.1 `DeviceState.withIdentity` — and why revision does not move
```kotlin
// session/DeviceState.kt:92   (KDoc :81-91, added by Phase 4, ADR-P4-002)
fun withIdentity(next: DeviceIdentity): DeviceState = copy(identity = next)
```
Phase 4's only edit to a Phase 1 type (13 lines). Every sibling mutator bumps the revision and restamps the time — `withCapability` (`:60-73`), `withCapabilities` (`:75-76`), `withBattery` (`:78-79`), `withAudio` (`:94-95`) each pass `revision = revision + 1, lastUpdatedEpochMillis = atEpochMillis`, and `attemptConnection` does the same on a legal move (`:122-128`). `withIdentity` does neither: it takes no time parameter, moves no revision, and leaves `lastUpdatedEpochMillis` as it found it.

The reason is at `:86-89` and it is a race argument, not a tidiness one: `applyIfNewer` (`:138-139`) adopts a candidate only when `candidate.revision > revision`. If learning a name raised the revision, an identity fill-in could beat a real connection change that happened first — a late `DeviceIdentity` merge would win an ordering contest it has no business entering. The caller is constrained by construction: `TrackedDeviceSession.withMergedIdentity` (`:89-92`) writes `session.copy(identity = next)` and `state.withIdentity(next)` in one expression, and `next` comes from `DeviceIdentity.mergedWith` (`device/DeviceIdentity.kt:63-68`), which is fill-only (`fillIfUnknown`, `:70`), so an established name is never restated — pinned behaviourally by `aNameArrivingLateFillsIdentityAndADifferentNameLaterNeverRestatesIt` (`DeviceSessionEngineTest.kt:228-241`) and structurally by `mergingIdentityUpdatesBothRecordsOrNeither` (`SessionStatePurityTest.kt:95-104`). `withIdentity`'s only caller in the whole tree is `TrackedDeviceSession.kt:91`.

⚠ Two honest limits. **No test asserts `withIdentity`'s revision behaviour**: `DeviceStateTest` (12 cases, `core/src/test/kotlin/com/omnibuds/core/session/DeviceStateTest.kt`) exercises `revision` for every other mutator (`:151-163`) and never calls `withIdentity`, so the claim rests on reading `DeviceState.kt:92`, where `copy()` names only `identity` (§18.13). ## 14. Resource-cleanup and leak rules

1. **There is no registration to dispose.** Phase 4 opens no receiver, no profile proxy, no channel and no coroutine; the engine's whole footprint is five private fields (`DeviceSessionEngine.kt:82-86`), which is rule 5.1 delivered by construction rather than by teardown code.
2. **The one cancellation path still publishes.** `terminateAll` runs inside `withContext(NonCancellable)` (`:206`) so the final value reaches consumers even when the caller's job is already cancelled — the reason Phase 3 needed the same escape.
3. **Cancellation is rethrown, not consumed** (`:121`), so the caller's structured concurrency is unaffected by the engine's bookkeeping.
4. **A stopped engine publishes an empty list with a reason attached**, not a frozen room: `tracked = emptyList()` (`:212`) with `SessionObservationStatus.Stopped` (`:214`) — SEC-ID-005's active-session row ("cleared with the session; no durable record").
5. **Nothing is retained past the session.** No event store, no snapshot history, no `refusedMoves` growth beyond 8 (`:446`), no lineage pointer from a re-minted id (§9's R4).
6. **Terminated sessions leave the list rather than staying in it flagged** (`applyAbsence` returns `null`, `:349`; `applyReport` returns `null`, `:268`), so `activeSessions` is never a filter over history — which is ADR-P4-006's third alternative refused, and the reason `termination` is never observed non-null (§18.7).
7. **A restart is legal and is not a repair.** After `consume` completes, `apply` builds fresh sessions from an emptied store (`DeviceSessionEngineTest.kt:462-472`), and `revision` continues from where it stood rather than resetting — a consumer holding the old value can still tell it is older.

## 15. Identity, redaction, and the two things this layer may not claim

**Identity.** `sessionId` is minted from a counter (`:397`) and carries no device content, asserted against an address substring and a name substring at `DeviceSessionEngineTest.kt:474-483`. The join to a device is held as the typed `DeviceObservationKey` and never read out as text (`TrackedDeviceSession.kt:30-32`). Where the platform reported no key, the session is `AMBIGUOUS`, published and counted but never matched — `expireAmbiguousSessions` runs before the fold (`:143`) so an unkeyed session cannot be carried into a second round even by accident, and two unkeyed records in one round stay two sessions (`:171-179`; `twoUnkeyedDevicesInOneRoundStayTwoSessionsBecauseNothingCanTellThemApart`, `DeviceSessionEngineTest.kt:215-226`). A name participates in no identity decision: `identityOf` fills identity only (`:511-512`), and `twoDevicesCarryingTheSameNameStayTwoSessions` proves the join ignores it (`:86-94`).

**Redaction.** `SessionIdentityChanged` carries counts, not values (`DeviceSessionEvent.kt:85-90`); every other payload is an id, a state, an enum, or Phase 3's error. `SessionStatePurityTest.kt:166-189` renders one session, all seven event cases and one raw key and asserts no MAC-shaped text. ⚠ What it does **not** prove is stated in §17.3 and §18.3: a display name reaches this layer unchanged from Phase 3 and is printed by the `data class` `toString()` of `TrackedDeviceSession`, because `displayName` lives inside `session.identity` (`:57-58`, `device/DeviceIdentity.kt:30`).

**Two claims this layer cannot make.** It may not claim a device is *usable* — that is ADR-P4-003's unreachable quartet (§2.6) and ADR-P4-008's refusal of `SESSION_ACTIVATED`. It may not claim a device is *saved* — every session is `TEMPORARY` (`:402-406`), `save()` is never called (`beingObservedNeverPromotesASessionIntoASavedRecord` pins the classification both ways, `SessionStatePurityTest.kt:246-254`), `DeviceRepository` is implemented nowhere, and the layer map refuses the import (`DependencyDirectionTest.kt:37-50, 256-264`, whose production-implementation guard is `noProductionClassImplementsTheProtocolOrRepositoryContracts` at `:237-251`). `PhaseFourScopeTest.kt:54-64` pins the same boundary from the text side.

### 17.3 What the guards cannot see
1. **The control-seam scan reads only `core/session`'s main sources.** `sessionMainSources()` (`PhaseFourScopeTest.kt:113-121`) is one relative path: it does not read the area's test sources, any other area, the platform module, or `di/OmniBudsBluetooth.kt`, where the engine is constructed (`:152-154`) — so a future control seam reaching the observer from the composition root is invisible to it.
2. **It matches strings, not types.** The forbidden-name check is `contains("com.omnibuds.core.platform.<Name>")` on code lines (`:46`), so it catches an explicit import and a fully qualified reference, but a wildcard import followed by a bare use of `ConnectedDeviceObserver` would pass, as would a `suspend () -> Unit` or `Flow<ConnectedDeviceSnapshot>`-shaped seam handed in from outside (ADR-P4-011's rejected `recovery` alternative would have been invisible to it — the reason the alternative was rejected rather than banned).
3. **Field checks are name-based reflection.** `noSessionTypeCanClaimVerificationBeyondWhatThePhaseHas` reads `TrackedDeviceSession::class.java.declaredFields` (`SessionStatePurityTest.kt:208`): names only, declared-only (a property's backing field, never a computed `get`), no supertypes, no parameter types, and JVM-only — a target without JVM reflection cannot run the guard at all. Nothing checks field *types*, so a `Boolean` field named `isActive` would pass. The textual scans in `PhaseFourScopeTest` are reflection-free by contrast, which is why the two files are complements rather than duplicates.
4. **No concurrency is measured.** Every case runs on `runTest`'s single-threaded scheduler (`DeviceSessionEngineTest.kt:50, 431-449`), so `Mutex` contention, an interleaving of `apply` with `consume`'s cancellation, and the "rapid `CONNECTED/DISCONNECTED`" sequence ADR-P4-009's prompt §14 asks about are exercised as *ordered rounds*, never as two threads. ADR-P4-009's own consequences sentence states this as unsolved, and this file records it as a guard limit: **nothing in the build measures multi-thread contention.**
5. **A name is not an address.** `assertNoAddressText` matches `([0-9A-Fa-f]{2}:){5}[0-9A-Fa-f]{2}` (`:290`); the fixture's own display name at `:168` is `"Address-Bearing"`, which passes while being printed. §18.3.
6. **Nothing checks the prose.** A dangling KDoc link (`TrackedDeviceSession.kt:24`, §18.15), a count that has fallen behind an enum (`SessionObservationStatus.kt:16`, `SessionVocabulary.kt:49`, `DeviceSessionEvent.kt:70`, §18.4-5), or an ADR line citation (§18.14) compiles clean.
7. **Nothing observes the run.** No counter of dropped events, no assertion that `tryEmit` returned false, no measurement of how long the lock is held, and no composition-root test — the engine is never driven by a real `ConnectedDeviceObserver` in any executing test (`DeviceSessionEngineTest` feeds hand-built projections).
8. **No device, ever.** Tier T1 (ADR-P4-012): the four categories a handset can report and the two link states it may never deliver are, as of this commit, unconfirmed by anything except Phase 3's research file.

## 18. Open items: where a claim rests on reading

1. ⚠ **The copy-bypass is still open, on purpose.** `DeviceState.kt:30-36` states that `copy(connection = …)` can bypass `attemptConnection` for anyone holding the record, and that narrowing the constructor does not help because Kotlin publishes a data class's `copy()` regardless. The engine is the only writer **by construction** — nothing else is handed `tracked` (`DeviceSessionEngine.kt:84`, private; `snapshot` is `asStateFlow()` at `:91`) — not by compiler. Confining mutation to one owner remains Phase 24's obligation, and ADR-P4-001's consequences say so precisely so this engine is not mistaken for that work. No check in the tree can see an end-run through `copy`.
2. ⚠ **ADR-P4-003's title and the code's set differ by two members.** The title reads "five states plus `ERROR`" and the decision sentence lists seven names; `reachableStates` declares **six** (`TrackedDeviceSession.kt:131-134`, filtered at `:133`) and the test pins `ERROR`'s absence (`SessionStatePurityTest.kt:57`), while `attemptConnection` still accepts `ERROR` from every state (`ConnectionState.kt:32, 101`). The reconcilable reading is that `ERROR` is *reachable in the type but requested by no engine path* — true, and true only of §4's codomain, which contains no `ERROR`. This file states the narrower claim because the narrower one is checkable.
3. ⚠ **A display name reaches the session layer unchanged from Phase 3 and is printable.** `identityOf` passes `report.displayName` straight through (`DeviceSessionEngine.kt:511-512`), it lands in `session.identity.displayName` (`device/DeviceIdentity.kt:30`), and `TrackedDeviceSession.displayName` reads it back (`TrackedDeviceSession.kt:57-58`). Because the type is a `data class`, its generated `toString()` prints it. Phase 3 already recorded the same property for `DeviceObservation.toString()` (its `specs.md` §17.2) and the same mitigation — there is no sink yet (ADR-P1-019), and the duty at emission belongs to a later phase. Nothing here can be blamed on Phase 4 except the *new* copy of the exposure, which ADR-P4-005's consequence ("`TrackedDeviceSession.toString()` can be printed in a test failure without leaking a device") states more strongly than the guard at §17.2 supports: it is true of identifiers, not of names.
4. ⚠ **`SessionObservationStatus`'s KDoc says "four cases" where the type has five** (`:16` against `:38, 44, 54, 63, 66`). The fifth is `Unread`, which `of` never returns (`:82-90`) and which exists as the value before the first round — the distinction the KDoc draws between "no projection yet" and "a projection that has not settled" is real, and the sentence counting it is stale. The test that pins the difference is named `…FourCasesExclusive` (`SessionStatePurityTest.kt:120`).
5. **Closed by the same deletion that rewrote item 7.** This item caught two sentences saying "four reasons" while the enum held five: `SessionVocabulary.kt`'s own header and `DeviceSessionEvent.kt:70` ("one of the four reasons in `SessionTermination`"). `AMBIGUOUS_SESSION_EXPIRED` had been added underneath the prose without re-counting it. `SessionTermination.ABSENT_FROM_COMPLETE_UNION` was then deleted at close-out (`e68c808`), so both sentences are now true and the enum holds exactly four members in the order the prose lists them - which is the right outcome but not a lucky one: what made it detectable was `terminationReasonsDescribeEngineDecisionsAndNeverADeviceAct` pinning the names in declaration order rather than counting them, so a silent fifth member now fails a test instead of only a paragraph.
6. ⚠ **One published round is an unvalidated policy choice on a handset.** ADR-P4-006 replaced a time window with a round window because a timer makes state depend on scheduling, and the choice is deterministic and testable (§9). It is still a guess about a phone: if announcements are slow (research U-4), a device that blipped could be re-created under a new id between two censuses; if the union is answered but the device enumeration lags a profile by one round, a live device gets `DISCONNECTED` for one round and reappears as a `SessionReconnected`. Nothing in Phase 4 measures a real gap distribution, and no handset run has occurred.
7. **Closed at close-out by deletion rather than by documentation.** This item used to list four unreachable code paths and two unread enum members: `TrackedDeviceSession.terminated`/`termination`/`isLive`/`withSession`, `SessionTimeline.endedAtEpochMillis`/`closedAt`, and `SessionTermination.ABSENT_FROM_COMPLETE_UNION`. All six were **deleted** (`e68c808`), because the engine drops a terminated session in the same round that publishes its end, so every one of them was a field or function whose only writer was code that never ran — the same refusal Phase 3 applied to its own `DEDUPED` observation kind (ADR-P2-018), and now recorded as a dated correction inside ADR-P4-002 rather than left as decoration with a warning comment. What remains genuinely unread in this layer is narrower and is not a defect: `DeviceState.applyIfNewer` has no production caller because Phase 4 has one writer (ADR-P4-009, and Phase 6 is where it becomes load-bearing), and `omniBudsDeviceSessionEngine` is constructed by no test (§18.13, RISK-075).
8. **Both `UNKNOWN`-link rows are now pinned, and the handset question they raise is unchanged.** This item used to read "two mapping rows have no test": row 6b (observable, `link == UNKNOWN`, tracked) and row 6c (the same evidence, untracked) are now exercised by `aTrackedDeviceWhoseLinkReadingVanishesIsRefusedRatherThanRewritten` and `anUntrackedDeviceWithNoBondAndNoLinkReadingOpensNothing` (`DeviceSessionEngineTest.kt:536-556`). The first is the more interesting result: `CONNECTED → UNKNOWN` has no row in `ConnectionStateTransitions` (`ConnectionState.kt:54-59`), so the engine records a refusal and holds the connected state rather than rewriting it — a missing reading does not become a state change. Whether a real stack ever lets a link reading vanish while still reporting the device observable is U-4/U-9 territory and unverified.
9. ⚠ **There is no executable composition root.** `omniBudsDeviceSessionEngine` (`di/OmniBudsBluetooth.kt:152-154`) is constructed by no test and called by no source file; `DeviceSessionEngine` and `ConnectedDeviceObserver` never meet in an executing test, where every case builds projections by hand (`DeviceSessionEngineTest.kt:527-544`). Phase 3's record made the same admission about its own factory (its `specs.md` §13, "called from no source file in the tree"), so the honest statement of Phase 4's integration proof is: **type-level only** — `consume`/`apply` accept exactly what `snapshot` publishes.
10. ⚠ **The wiring in that function's KDoc is prose, not code.** The two `launch` lines at `di/OmniBudsBluetooth.kt:139-146` sit in a ` ```text ` fence, so they are not compiled, not type-checked, and not covered by `allWarningsAsErrors`. Read as documentation of a shape the phase deliberately declines to build — a `coroutineScope { launch { … } }` would hand the engine a scope, which rule 5.1 and ADR-P4-009 refuse.
11. ⚠ **Sessions grow unbounded when the union never answers.** A keyed session is removed only by an answered-absence past grace (`:343-349`), a repeated `DISCONNECTED` report past grace (`:262-269`), a stopped projection (`:332-339`) or a teardown (`:209-212`); under `Refused` or `Unconfirmed` the rule is `else -> session` (`:377`), which is exactly what ADR-P4-007 requires and exactly what an app that keeps a refused permission in place for a day will accumulate. There is no cap on `tracked`, no eviction, and no test that applies more than a handful of sessions (`KEY_LEFT/KEY_RIGHT/KEY_MID`, `DeviceSessionEngineTest.kt:613-619`). `refusals` is bounded (`:446`); the store is not.
12. ⚠ **The engine cannot detect a lying projection, and inherits RISK-049.** Every decision reads Phase 3's axes as given: `targetStateOf` trusts `availability`, `link` and `bond` (`:481-500`), `applyAbsence` trusts `isUnionComplete` (`SessionObservationStatus.kt:87`), and nothing cross-checks two rounds that disagree about the same key or asks whether a "complete" union was actually enumerated. Phase 3's answer to that class of failure — ADR-P3-019's answerability-ladder correction, recorded as `RISK-049`'s sibling — was a *projection-side* fix; Phase 4 sits downstream of it with no new sensor. What Phase 4 does add is that the projection can no longer be read as a device claim (§4), which bounds the damage without bounding the trust.
13. **Half closed; the other half is the design.** `withIdentity`'s revision behaviour is now pinned by `anIdentityFillIsNotAStateMoveAndNeverMovesTheRevision` (`SessionStatePurityTest.kt`), which asserts that the revision does not move, that the connection is untouched, and - the part that matters for Phase 6 - that `applyIfNewer` treats an identity fill as *not newer*. `applyIfNewer` itself still has no production caller, and ADR-P4-009 now says so deliberately rather than claiming a gate: with one writer there is no competing candidate to filter. It is not dead code, it is the seam the next writer must pass through, and the test above is what keeps its meaning stable until then.
14. ⚠ **ADR-P4-008's flow-rule citation is off by one line.** It reads `docs/phases/phase-0/specs.md:146-147` for "rules 5.5/5.6"; the flow/buffering clause is at `:145` and the StateFlow-not-commands clause at `:146`, with `:147` being rule 5.7. The rules themselves are quoted correctly, and the KDoc in `DeviceSessionEvent.kt:27` cites them by number ("rules 5.5-5.6") without a line, which is the form that still resolves.
15. ⚠ **A KDoc link names a member that does not exist.** `TrackedDeviceSession.kt:24` says `[state] is updated only through [movedTo], and [movedTo] only through [DeviceState.attemptConnection]`; the companion function is `moving` (`:123`) and no `movedTo` appears anywhere in the tree. The claim it makes is true of `moving` → `mapState` → `withState` (`:128, 139, 77`), so the sentence is right and the reference is wrong.
16. **Closed at close-out: same-key-within-one-round is merged, not won by order.** `keyedReports[record.key] = record` used to overwrite, so a projection that carried one device twice left the session decided by which row came last - arriving at the ordering-by-accident that ADR-P4-009 refuses for timestamps. The fold now applies Phase 3's own rank, `DeviceObservation.mergedWith` (`platform/DeviceObservation.kt:103-118`), which is total on joinable keys and refuses unjoinable ones, so `CONNECTED` and `DISCONNECTED` rows for one device in one round resolve to the live link and the profile sets union. Pinned from both directions by `twoReportsOfOneKeyInOneRoundAreMergedAndNeverChosenByArrivalOrder`. The `else` branch of the same fold - two *unkeyed* rows in one round - stays two sessions, which is item 5's rule and prompt section 12's prohibition, not an oversight.
