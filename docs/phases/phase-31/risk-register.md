# Phase 31 — Risk Register

## R-31-01: Overstated compatibility
**Likelihood:** High | **Impact:** High
**Mitigation:** Scoped verdicts; evidence levels; no auto-promotion.

## R-31-02: Silent regressions
**Likelihood:** Medium | **Impact:** High
**Mitigation:** Baseline comparison; immutable baselines.

## R-31-03: Cross-profile leakage
**Likelihood:** Low | **Impact:** High
**Mitigation:** Isolation; per-profile planning; no shared state.

## R-31-04: Fabricated device support
**Likelihood:** Medium | **Impact:** High
**Mitigation:** Synthetic marked; hardware claims need evidence.

## R-31-05: Skipped tests as passes
**Likelihood:** Medium | **Impact:** Medium
**Mitigation:** Dispositions distinct; skipped → partial at best.
